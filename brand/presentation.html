What we heard

The brief

A free Windows utility by CafeTako that turns the top edge of the screen into a small notch for music, lyrics, focus timers, water, to-dos and files. It should feel calm, light and friendly, never like a dashboard.

calmlightfriendlyfluid

Success: reads at 16 px in the system tray, works in one colour, carries the mascot Sui, and stays distinct from generic 'dynamic island' pills.

SuiNotch — identity concepts2
Concept A

Sui in the notch

The notch itself, with Sui (sui = water) living inside it.

  • The pill is the product: the mark is literally the notch at the top of your screen
  • Sui gives the app a character that also appears as the idle mascot
  • Droplet sides at exactly 60°, one pill radius, a small-size cut without eyes for 16-32 px
  • The wordmark's i carries a droplet instead of a dot
