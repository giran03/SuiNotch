# SuiNotch 1.0.0

The first release of SuiNotch, a living notch for the top of your screen. Every feature is included, free.

### Added
- Music: artwork, seek bar, playback controls, volume and synced lyrics from LRCLIB, with a lyrics pill under the notch.
- Focus timer with Pomodoro sessions, short and long breaks.
- Water tracker with a daily goal, hourly chart and gentle reminders.
- To-do list with colours, due times, snooze and daily progress.
- Calendar with task dots.
- Shelf: drop files or text on the notch, drag them back out later.
- Overlays for volume, brightness, Caps Lock, keyboard language, battery, Wi-Fi and headphones.
- Sui, the mascot who lives in the notch, blinks, dozes and waves.
- Settings for size, display, accent colour, roundness, animation style and every feature.
- Automatic updates from GitHub Releases, installed only while you're away from the PC.
- What's new: these notes open once after each install or update, and from Settings > About.

### Performance
- Animations follow your display's refresh rate (60, 120, 144, 165 Hz and more) with very little CPU use.

### Fixed
- A task's time and delete buttons stay visible while you move between them or edit the task.
