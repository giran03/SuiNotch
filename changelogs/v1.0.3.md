# SuiNotch 1.0.3

### Added
- Lyrics fallback: when LRCLIB has no lyrics for a song, SuiNotch now asks a backup lyrics service, and "Search again" can reach its results too. Only the song title and artist are sent, and it can be turned off in Settings > Music and lyrics > Privacy.
- Synced lyrics first: if LRCLIB only has plain lyrics, SuiNotch checks the fallback for a synced version before settling for them.
- Saved lyrics: songs you have looked up are kept on this PC, so their lyrics show at once and work offline. You can turn it off or clear them in Settings.
- Lyrics source: the lyrics panel says whether the lyrics came from LRCLIB or a backup service and whether they are synced.
- Keep searching: when a song has no lyrics at first, SuiNotch searches again before giving up. Set how many retries (0 to 10, default 3) in Settings > Music and lyrics.
- Translated titles: a song the player lists in Latin letters, such as Kan Saete Kuyashiiwa, is also searched under its native title and artist (勘冴えて悔しいわ), found through Apple's music store in Japan. Both names are tried in each search round, so they count as one retry. This is part of the lyrics fallback setting.
- Lyrics look: the closed notch's lyric line is made of choices that combine freely, in Settings > Music and lyrics, with a live preview above them. Placement (under the notch, or in it in place of the song title), Backdrop (black pill, album tint or none), Highlight (a fill that follows the singing, by line or word by word, available everywhere), Text colour, Text size, how New lines arrive (slide, fade, typewriter or none), what Long lines do (shrink, scroll or cut) and whether the line stays, dimmed, When paused. The Pill, In the notch, Text only, Karaoke and Glow looks are one-click presets, and an earlier style choice carries over. The karaoke fill now follows how long a line takes to sing instead of creeping through breaks.
- Word by word: the Words highlight fills each word as it is sung, in the notch and on the lyrics panel's current line. Where a backup service has the song's word timing (many English songs) it is exact, and the panel says "word-synced"; other lines and songs are timed from their text, by syllable for English and by character or kana for Chinese, Japanese and Korean. It is only asked for when Words is chosen, under the lyrics fallback setting.
- Safer matching: word timing is checked the way a listener would before it is used. If most lines would have to be sung faster than anyone sings, or the words sit outside their own lines, or the lines are evenly spaced or run past the song, it is thrown away and the estimate is used, and one bad line only loses its own words. Lyrics for a live, remix, cover, instrumental, acoustic, sped-up or slowed version are no longer used for the studio song, or the other way round.
- Second line: the lyric can show the next line, romaji (Latin letters), a translation, or Auto (translation, else romaji) under the current line, or after it in the notch when there is room. The pill only grows taller when the song has what you asked for, and the setting says whether the current song does. The open lyrics panel can show romaji or a translation under every line too, with a small Aあ button to switch it. Songs found on LRCLIB, which has neither, get them from a backup service when the fallback is on.
- Tooltips: rest the pointer on a button that isn't self-explanatory (page dots, the player switch, the lyrics and recently played buttons, lyric timing, mute, undo and a few more) and a short hint fades in. Moving to the next button slides it across. Turn them off in Settings > General.
- Web images: an image dragged from a browser onto the notch is saved as a file on the shelf, with its name, instead of as a link. Nothing is downloaded; the browser hands over the picture itself, and it is copied before the browser can delete it. Files saved this way are removed once they are off the shelf and SuiNotch restarts or the shelf is cleared.
- Recently played: a new button on the Music page shows the songs you played lately. Click one to copy its name.
- Follow the mouse: in Settings > Display, the notch can move to whichever screen your pointer rests on.
- Settings search: type anywhere in the Settings window to find a setting, then click a result to open its page.
- Reset all settings: About > Your data puts every setting back to a fresh install's after a second press to confirm. Tasks, the shelf and the water log are kept.
- Diagnostics: Settings > About > Diagnostics shows your monitors, what SuiNotch thinks is full screen, the music player, saved lyrics and the end of the log, with a button to copy it all for a bug report.
- Sounds: Settings > Sounds has eight built-in reminder sounds (Sui Drop, Bell, Soft, Glass, Ripple, Spark, Beacon and Classic Ring) that you can preview with Play and Stop, and assign separately to focus, breaks, timers, tasks, calendar events, water and low battery, or set to Silent. A master mute and volume cover all of them. Your earlier sound choices carry over.
- Your own sounds: import a WAV, MP3 or OGG file (up to 20 MB and five minutes) with Browse or by dropping it on the Sounds page, then rename it, trim its start and length (up to 30 seconds), even out its loudness, replace its file or delete it. Imports are copied to this PC and nothing is uploaded. If one goes missing, the reminder plays its default chime.
- Timer ringing: a timer can keep ringing for a set time, with an adjustable pause between rings and a volume that rises gradually.

### Changed
- Calendar tasks keep their selected day visible in To-do, with a list for that day and optional reminders. Add several tasks to the same day, move between days, or return to Calendar without losing a draft. Removing a reminder keeps the task on its calendar day.
- Full-screen detection looks at the topmost window covering your screen, so borderless full-screen games hide the notch even when another window has focus. Overlays, the taskbar and Alt-Tab are ignored, and the check repeats every couple of seconds.
- The volume control now opens as a vertical slider above the speaker icon, so it no longer covers the playback buttons.
- Settings descriptions use the space next to each control and wrap to a second line instead of being cut off, including on rows with a button group.
- Lyric choices that don't apply, such as the backdrop with lyrics in the notch, are dimmed in Settings instead of doing nothing.
- When a player gives no cover (many browser tabs), the music page and the closed notch show a music-note placeholder in your accent colour instead of a faint grey box.
- New items on the shelf are added at the far left, and the oldest are the ones dropped when the shelf is full.
- A fresh install's accent colour follows Windows by default. Existing installs keep their choice.
- The app icon no longer sits on a dark tile; it is transparent.

### Fixed
- A song that stopped playing and then resumed was sometimes not shown in Now playing again.
- Dragging a shelf or clipboard item out now folds the notch away, so it no longer covers where you drop.
