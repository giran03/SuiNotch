# SuiNotch 1.0.2

### Added
- Notch style: choose how the notch meets the top of your screen in Settings > Appearance. Docked blends into the edge as before, Floating hovers below it as a rounded capsule, and Flat sits square against it.
- Floating gap: set how far a floating notch hovers from the screen edge, from 4 to 24 px. Moving the mouse to the very top of the screen still opens it.
