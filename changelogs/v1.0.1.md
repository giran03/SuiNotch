# SuiNotch 1.0.1

### Added
- When more than one app is playing media, a switch button appears next to the lyrics button, with a count of open players. Click it to move between Spotify, your browser and other players; the player's name shows under the artist. Your pick is remembered and chosen again when that player reopens.
- Focus: a plain timer with quick presets and a stopwatch with laps, next to the Pomodoro.
- Stats page: this week's focus time, the days you met your water goal and the tasks you finished, with streaks.
- Clipboard history on the Shelf: the last texts and images you copy, 10 by default and up to 50 in Settings > Shelf. Click one to copy it again or drag it into any app. Copies that password managers mark as private are never kept. Each new copy shows briefly in the notch, which you can turn off in Settings > HUD and popups.
- Calendar events: paste an Outlook, Google or other iCal link in Settings > Calendar to see your events on the Calendar page, with a heads-up 10 minutes before each one.
- Lyrics: nudge the timing earlier or later for each song, and Search again when the lyrics are wrong or missing.
- Sui reactions: Sui cheers when you finish a focus session, reach your water goal or clear today's tasks, and naps through long breaks. Turn it off in Settings > General.
- Display: Follow the active window moves the notch to the screen you're working on.
- Appearance: Use the Windows accent colour, which follows the colour you set in Windows.
- About: a Buy us a coffee button to support the developers.

### Changed
- While music plays, the closed notch shows the song title and artist between the cover and the equalizer.
- The Settings window follows Corner roundness from Appearance, its window corners included.
- Settings with a row of buttons have more room between the label and the buttons.
- Reminders wait while Windows is in Do Not Disturb, presenting or running a full-screen game, and chimes stay silent. Turn this off in Settings > General.
- Focus: picking another duration while a timer is running or paused no longer resets it. The timer keeps going, its button shows a dot and the time left, and Start or Back decide what happens next.
- If SuiNotch ever crashes, the cause is now written to suinotch.log in its data folder, so problems are easier to report.
