# SuiNotch 1.0.1

### Added
- When more than one app is playing media, a switch button appears next to the lyrics button, with a count of open players. Click it to move between Spotify, your browser and other players; the player's name shows under the artist.

### Changed
- If SuiNotch ever crashes, the cause is now written to suinotch.log in its data folder, so problems are easier to report.
